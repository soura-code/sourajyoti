Beyond Math

Outside of mathematics, I am interested in literature, music, movies and paintings. I love to hike, visit new places and attend concerts or theaters in my free time.

I grew up in West Bengal, India. As a kid and a teenager, I voraciously read Bengali literature, often to the extreme of reading 19th-century Bengali classics at a premature age. In my later teenage years, I also developed a serious enthusiasm about theaters. I even took part as an actor in some of the theatrical productions of my school and college, sometimes getting recognition within the institute. I am also an avid admirer of humanitarian movies from all over the world.

I occasionally indulge in writing poetry and blogs in Bengali, which I plan to upload to my website in the future.
