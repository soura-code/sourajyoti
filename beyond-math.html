Beyond Math

Outside of mathematics, I am interested in literature, music, film and painting. In my free time, I love to hike, explore new places, and go to concerts and the theater.

I grew up in West Bengal, India. As a child and a teenager, I read Bengali literature voraciously, sometimes picking up 19th-century Bengali classics well before I was old enough to fully appreciate them. In my later teens, I developed a serious passion for theater and acted in several productions at my school and college, a few of which earned recognition within the institution.

I am also an avid admirer of parallel cinema, social realism and humanist drama from around the world. Some of my favorite directors include Satyajit Ray, Ritwik Ghatak, Mrinal Sen, Woody Allen and Abbas Kiarostami.

I occasionally write poetry and blog posts in Bengali, which I hope to share here someday.

Reading Group on Ambedkar and Contemporary Dalit Thought

In Fall 2026, I am part of the reading group on Ambedkar and Contemporary Dalit Thought, recognized and funded by the University of Connecticut Humanities Institute. This semester, we are reading and talking about:

  • B. R. Ambedkar, Annihilation of Caste
  • Kancha Ilaiah, Why I Am Not a Hindu
  • Gail Omvedt, Dalits and the Democratic Revolution
  • B. R. Ambedkar, Against the Madness of Manu, and “The Rise and Fall of the Hindu Woman: Who Was Responsible for It?”
  • Isabel Wilkerson, Caste: The Origins of Our Discontents
  • Film screening: Fandry (dir. Nagraj Manjule)
