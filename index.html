Sourajyoti Maiti

PhD Candidate · Department of Mathematics · University of Connecticut

I am a PhD candidate at the University of Connecticut, advised by Prof. Kyu-Hwan Lee. I work in computational number theory and on applications of machine learning to pure mathematics.

Before UConn, I earned an M.Sc. in Mathematics from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute (RKMVERI), ranking first in the department. I did my B.Sc. (Honours) in Mathematics at St. Xavier’s College, Kolkata.

Research

Ongoing project

Optimal models and gonality of modular curves using reinforcement learning

Searching for optimal affine models of modular curves such as X1(N) and studying their gonality, using reinforcement learning to guide the search.

Interests: computational number theory and machine learning applications in pure mathematics.
