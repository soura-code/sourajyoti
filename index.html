Sourajyoti Maiti

PhD Candidate in Mathematics, University of Connecticut

I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Connecticut, where I am advised by Prof. Kyu-Hwan Lee. I joined UConn as a graduate student in Fall 2024 and became a PhD candidate in April 2026.

My research interests are in computational number theory and machine learning applications in pure mathematics. My current project studies optimal models and the gonality of modular curves using reinforcement learning.

Before coming to UConn, I received my M.Sc. in Mathematics from Ramakrishna Mission Vivekananda Educational and Research Institute (RKMVERI), where I ranked first in the department, and my B.Sc. with Honours in Mathematics from St. Xavier’s College (Autonomous), Kolkata.
